Questions

Good to know.

What is the Chief of Staff?

One helper that lives in the AI you already use and takes on the little jobs: the shop, the bills, your files, research, posts and your day. It does each one and checks with you before anything final.

Can I get help getting started?

Yes. When your place comes up, we help you add it to your AI and get the first jobs going.

Which AI do I need?

The one you already use. If you use ChatGPT, you are set. Most other AI assistants work too.

What else do I need?

Your email and calendar connected to your AI. Each job adds what it needs, such as an online account with a store you already use for the shop.

Will it spend my money?

No. The Grocer fills your cart and stops. The Bill Clerk tracks and reminds. Paying is always your click.

Do you see my files?

Your files stay in your own Google Drive, and we only look at what you choose to share with us. We never ask for your passwords.

How does the waitlist work?

It is free. Places go in order of joining, and each week we email you your place in line. Members get invites to share with friends.

Can it take on other jobs?

Tell us on the waitlist form what you would hand it. We add new jobs as we go, starting with the ones people ask for most.
