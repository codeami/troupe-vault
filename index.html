Questions

Good to know.

What is ThoughtSignal?

A set of helpers for small, regular jobs: the shop, the bills, your files, research, posts and your day. Each one does one job well and checks with you before anything final.

Which AI do I need?

The one you already use. If you use ChatGPT, you are set. Most other AI assistants work too.

Will a helper spend my money?

No. The Grocer fills your cart and stops. The Bill Clerk tracks and reminds. Paying is always your click.

What does the $1 pre-order get me?

The basic skill pack, sent by email when early access opens, and a place near the front of the line. Ask before then and we refund your $1.

How does the waitlist work?

Places go in order of joining. Each week we email you your place in line. Members get invites to share with friends.

Do you see my files?

No. The helpers work in your own AI, on your own accounts and devices. We never ask for your passwords.

Can you set it up for me?

Yes. The done-for-you service sets the helpers up for you, or for your team, and keeps them running.
