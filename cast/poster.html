How it works

What The Poster does for you

“Send me a few photos from your week. I'll pick the best ones, crop them for each app, and write a caption that sounds like you, with a version for each place you post. I'll suggest when each one goes out. You read them, change what you like, and press post.”

When it works

Sunday evening: “plan my posts”. Or straight after an event, while the photos are fresh.

What you need first

A handful of photos on your phone or computer. Two or three of your past posts, so it learns how you sound. Works in the AI you already use, such as ChatGPT. It never posts on its own.

What's inside

What it can do
