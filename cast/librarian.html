How it works

What The Librarian does for you

“Point me at a drive, your photo library or a messy downloads folder. I'll list what's there, find the doubles and the clutter, and suggest a few simple folders. Nothing moves and nothing is deleted until you say yes. Then I write a short note for everything worth keeping, so later you can just ask where it is.”

When it works

One weekend afternoon for the first big sort, then a short tidy once a month: “tidy my files”.

What you need first

The computer that holds the files you want to sort. A backup first, on an external drive or in the cloud. A notes app you like. Works in the AI you already use, such as ChatGPT. Nothing is deleted without your yes.

What's inside

What it can do

  • 01

    Disk scan

    Maps what's on each drive: how much space, what kind of files, what's old and what's saved twice.

  • 02

    Double finder

    Finds the same photo or document saved more than once, and lists them for you to confirm.

  • 03

    Photo sort

    Groups photos by year, place and event, and sets aside blurry shots and old screenshots.

  • 04

    Folder plan

    Suggests a small set of folders that fits your life, and moves files only after you agree.

  • 05

    Second brain

    Writes a one-line note for each file worth keeping, so “where is the lease?” gets a straight answer.
