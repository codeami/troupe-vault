How it works

What The Grocer does for you

“Tell me what you need this week. I'll check the prices at the stores you use, swap in a cheaper size or brand when it's the same thing, and fill your cart. Then I stop. You look it over and pay.”

When it works

Once a week before your usual shop: “plan my shop”. Or any time you add to the list.

What you need first

Your shopping list, typed out or as a photo. An online account with one or two stores you already use. Works in the AI you already use, such as ChatGPT. It never pays: the last click is yours.

What's inside

What it can do

  • 01

    Weekly list

    Turns your notes, a photo or a quick voice message into one clean list, grouped the way the store is laid out.

  • 02

    Price check

    Compares each item across your stores by the kilo or the litre, and points out the deals worth taking.

  • 03

    Cart fill

    Puts the cheapest basket in your cart and leaves it there for you to check and pay.
