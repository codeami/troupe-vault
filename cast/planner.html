How it works

What The Planner does for you

“Tell me everything on your mind, in any order. I'll sort it into today, this week and later, put the fixed things on your calendar, and give you three to start with. In the evening I'll ask what got done and move the rest, no fuss.”

When it works

Each morning: “plan my day”. Each evening: “close my day”. A short review every Friday.

What you need first

A calendar and a notes or reminders app you already use. Ten minutes on the first day to tell it what matters to you. Works in the AI you already use, such as ChatGPT. The easiest place to start.
