How it works

What The Researcher does for you

“Give me a question, and a few links or videos if you have them. I'll find good sources, set them up in a research notebook, and hand you one page: what the sources agree on, where they differ, and what nobody knows yet. Every point comes with its source, so you can check it.”

When it works

Whenever a question needs more than one search: a big purchase, a new topic for work, a school project.

What you need first

A free Google account for NotebookLM. A question you care about. Works in the AI you already use, such as ChatGPT.

What's inside

What it can do

  • 01

    Source hunt

    Finds a handful of good sources, says why each is worth reading, and skips the thin ones.

  • 02

    Notebook setup

    Puts the sources into a NotebookLM notebook, named and ready for your questions.

  • 03

    One-page brief

    Writes the short version: what's agreed, what's argued, and what to read next.

  • 04

    Listen later

    Sets up a short audio summary you can play on a walk or on the way to work.
