How it works

What the Chief of Staff does for you

“Tell me what is on your mind this week, in any order. I'll sort it into the shop, the bills, your files, your reading, your posts and your plans, take each one as far as I safely can, and bring back only what needs your yes.”

When it works

Every morning: “what's on today”. And any time something comes to mind: “add this”.

What you need first

The AI you already use, such as ChatGPT, with your email and calendar connected. Each job adds what it needs, like an online account with your usual store for the shop. It never pays or sends anything without your click.
