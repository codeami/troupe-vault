How it works

What The Bill Clerk does for you

“Forward me your bills, or let me find them in your inbox. I'll write down each one, remind you before it's due, and tell you when a bill jumps for no clear reason. If a cheaper plan fits how you use it, I'll draft the email asking for it. I never pay anything myself.”

When it works

Each time a bill lands in your inbox, and a short summary on the first of every month.

What you need first

An email account where your power, water, gas, phone and internet bills arrive. A calendar for the reminders. Works in the AI you already use, such as ChatGPT. Paying stays with you.

What's inside

What it can do

  • 01

    Bill log

    Reads each bill and notes the amount, the usage and the due date in one simple sheet.

  • 02

    Due-date reminders

    Puts every due date on your calendar, with a nudge three days before.

  • 03

    Jump finder

    Compares this month with the last twelve and explains any jump in plain words.

  • 04

    Better plan

    Checks whether a cheaper plan fits how you use it, and drafts the email to your provider for you to send.
